# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and harassment-free community for everyone.

Contributors must communicate constructively, respect differing experience and viewpoints, accept
responsibility for mistakes, and focus feedback on the work rather than the person. Harassment,
threats, discriminatory language, doxxing, sexualized conduct, and sustained disruption are not
acceptable in project spaces.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict
participation when behavior is unsafe or disruptive. Report conduct concerns privately through the
maintainer contact channel configured on the GitHub repository. Do not disclose personal or
sensitive information in a public issue.

Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented privately, and avoid conflicts of
interest wherever possible.
